Q3 Launch: Dark Mode Everywhere

Edited just now · by K. Shuvo

Our editor stays blindingly white by default, which is rough on the eyes during long writing sessions. The brief below proposes shipping a true dark theme across every surface: docs, sheets and slides.

Note: the browser's built-in dark mode silently fails on this page. One click of the extension fixes it.
  • Reduce eye strain for night-time writers
  • Single GPU-composited filter with zero performance cost
  • Per-site ignore list for brand-locked pages
